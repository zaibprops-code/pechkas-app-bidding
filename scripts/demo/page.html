The same screens and the same database rules as the app on your phone, running in this page with made-up people and jobs. Nobody is told anything and nothing is sent anywhere.

What the page cannot do

  • No camera: choose a photo from your files instead.
  • No real map: a street drawing stands in.
  • No location: the demo puts you in F-11, or at the technician’s job.
  • No notifications, calls or WhatsApp.
