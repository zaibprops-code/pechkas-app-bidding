## Full description (4,000 characters at most)

```
Something broken at home? Tell Pechkas what it is, say what you will pay, and choose from technicians who offer to come.

HOW IT WORKS
• Show what is wrong: a voice note (Urdu is fine), a short video, photos, or a few words.
• Name your price. The app shows the Pechkas website's price for common jobs as a guide.
• Technicians who do that work in your sector send offers: your price, or theirs, and how soon they can be there.
• You choose. Only then does that technician get your address and number.

ONLY CHECKED TECHNICIANS
Before a technician gets a single job, Pechkas checks who he is, gives him a trial job and goes through the rules with him. Customers see "New" beside a technician until he has enough ratings — never stars nobody gave.

NO SURPRISES AT THE GATE
• A four-digit start code on your phone proves the person at your door is the one the app sent.
• The price is agreed in the app before he comes. If the job turns out bigger, he must send the new price with a reason and a photo, and nothing changes unless you approve it. Say no, and you pay only a small visit fee for his petrol, shown before you post.
• Parts he buys come with the shop receipt.
• Before and after photos of the work.

WHILE THE JOB IS ON
• See when he sets off and when he arrives.
• Message him in the app, or call.
• Send his name to your family on WhatsApp.
• An emergency button that tells Pechkas at once and shows the police, Rescue 1122, fire brigade and Edhi numbers.

AFTER THE JOB
• Pay him in cash. The app takes no payments and holds no money.
• Rate each other. Neither sees the other's rating until both have rated.
• Something wrong? Tell Pechkas within a day. Pechkas stands behind the work for 30 days.
• Happy with him? Ask for him again next time: your job goes to him first.

FOR TECHNICIANS
Work with Pechkas: the technician side is in Roman Urdu (with English if you prefer). See jobs in your trades and sectors, accept or send your price, and see exactly what you keep before you offer. Pechkas' fee is 10% of your labour, never of the parts you buy, and 7% when a customer you have worked for before books you again. Applying is free.

WHERE
Pechkas works in Islamabad, in F-11, E-11 and G-13 for now, with more sectors to come.

Pechkas — har pech ka hal.
```

> The sectors line must match the live sectors in the database. If you open
> a new sector, change this sentence too.
